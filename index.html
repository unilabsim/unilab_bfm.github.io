From fast training
to reliable motion.从高效训练
到可靠运动。

Five reproducible studies trace one path: scaling the BFM-0 foundation model, making SONIC training efficient on AMD accelerators, validating a full SONIC v1.0 reproduction, and — added in October — porting FlashMimic and Uni-SuperMimic training to ROCm under the same evidence standard.五个可复现研究项目串起同一条路径：扩展 BFM-0 基础模型，在 AMD 加速器上高效训练 SONIC，用透明证据验证 SONIC v1.0 的完整复现；十月新增 FlashMimic 与 Uni-SuperMimic 在 ROCm 上的适配与训练，沿用同一证据标准。
